BeTheHabit support

A little help for your habits.

Questions, a problem, or an idea for BeTheHabit? Contact Bryce, the app’s developer.

Email supportbryce7cs@gmail.com

Reporting a problem

Include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot can help, but remove any personal habit information you do not want to share. Never send your Apple ID password.

Using the Home Screen widget

Open BeTheHabit once after installation. Add its widget from your Home Screen, then touch and hold the widget and choose Edit Widget to select a habit. Check-in habits support check-in and undo; timer habits support start and stop. Open the app to log numeric progress.

Sharing a habit with a friend

Both people need BeTheHabit and an iCloud account. Open a habit’s details and choose Share with Friend. Only selected habits are shared, and your friend can view progress without editing it. Personal notes are excluded.

Sharing updates while the app is open. Your friend can pull down to refresh. If an update does not appear, check the internet connection and iCloud sign-in, then reopen BeTheHabit.

Your existing habits

Habits are stored on your device. Avoid deleting the app when troubleshooting a storage issue; contact support first if you need help preserving your data.
