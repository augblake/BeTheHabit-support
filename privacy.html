BeTheHabit privacy

Your habits, your choice.

This policy explains how BeTheHabit handles information when you track habits, use its widget, back up with iCloud, share a habit with a friend, or contact support.

Effective date: November 8, 2026 · Developer: Bryce Blake

Information stored on your device

BeTheHabit stores your habits, goals, progress history, personal notes, timer state, and app settings on your device. The app and its widget use a shared local storage container so that changes made in one can appear in the other. Local copies may also be included in device backups managed by Apple or by you.

iCloud backup

Starting with version 1.0.1, if you are signed in to iCloud, BeTheHabit automatically saves a backup in your private iCloud storage. The backup includes your habits, goals, progress history, personal notes, and app settings, along with the name you enter for sharing and which habits you share. It is used to restore your habits. Apple stores and processes this data under its iCloud terms, and we do not run a server that receives it.

Sharing a habit with a friend

Sharing is optional and happens only when you choose to share a habit. Shared data goes through Apple’s iCloud to the people you invite. It includes the name you enter and the shared habit, including its name, icon, goal, and progress history. It does not include your notes. The app shows friends your last three days of progress and your streak, but the habit’s progress history is sent to their device. Updates continue until you stop sharing, the friend removes the habit, or you delete the habit. A friend’s device may keep information it has already received.

Advertising and analytics

BeTheHabit does not include advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell your habit information. Apple may separately process App Store and diagnostic information according to your device settings and its policies.

Support email

If you email support, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond and troubleshoot. Do not include private habit details unless you want us to see them. Support correspondence is handled through our email provider, Google, and retained as needed to address your request and related follow-up. You can request deletion by contacting us.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit or delete habits in the app. BeTheHabit does not require an account with us and does not upload your habits to a developer server. Deleting the app removes its local app data, subject to Apple’s storage and backup behavior, but does not necessarily remove your iCloud backup or end sharing, so stop sharing first. iCloud data can be managed in your device’s iCloud settings or by contacting Apple. Device backups are managed by Apple or by you.

This support website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no analytics scripts, advertising, cookies set by our pages, or contact forms. GitHub may process visitors’ IP addresses and request information to operate and secure its hosting service. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

If BeTheHabit’s data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date.
