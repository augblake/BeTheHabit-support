BeTheHabit privacy

Your habits, your choice.

This policy explains how BeTheHabit handles information when you track habits, use its widget, share with friends, or contact support.

Effective date: October 9, 2026 · Developer: Bryce Blake

Information stored on your device

BeTheHabit stores your habits, goals, progress history, personal notes, timer state, and app settings on your device. The app and its widget use a shared local storage container so that changes made in one can appear in the other. Local copies may also be included in device backups managed by Apple or by you.

Optional iCloud friend sharing

If you choose to share a habit, BeTheHabit sends that selected habit’s information to Apple’s CloudKit service in your iCloud account. This includes your chosen display name, the habit’s name and settings, goals, progress history, timer state, and update time. Personal notes are removed before a shared snapshot is uploaded.

Sharing is private and invitation-based. People you invite can view the selected habit but cannot edit it through BeTheHabit. Shared snapshots are cached on recipients’ devices for offline viewing. The app also stores iCloud record identifiers locally to associate invitations and shared records with the correct iCloud account.

BeTheHabit’s developer does not operate a separate server for your habits. Apple processes iCloud data under its own privacy policy and service terms.

Advertising and analytics

BeTheHabit does not include advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell your habit information. Apple may separately process App Store and diagnostic information according to your device settings and its policies.

Support email

If you email support, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond and troubleshoot. Do not include private habit details unless you want us to see them. Support correspondence is handled through our email provider, Google, and retained as needed to address your request and related follow-up. You can request deletion by contacting us.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit or delete local habits in the app and manage sharing through Apple’s sharing controls. When you delete an owned shared habit, the app attempts to delete its shared CloudKit record during a successful sync. Internet access and an available iCloud account are needed to complete cloud changes.

Stopping sharing prevents further authorized access, but recipients’ offline caches may remain until their next successful refresh. It cannot remove copies or screenshots a recipient saved independently. Deleting the app removes its local app data subject to Apple’s storage and backup behavior; it does not itself request deletion of CloudKit records or existing backups.

This support website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no analytics scripts, advertising, cookies set by our pages, or contact forms. GitHub may process visitors’ IP addresses and request information to operate and secure its hosting service. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

If BeTheHabit’s data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date.
